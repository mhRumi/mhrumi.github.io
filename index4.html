About Me

Hi, I'm a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering with over two years of professional DevOps experience. Currently, I work at Shahjalal University of Science and Technology, where I teach courses in Cloud Computing, Software Project Management, and Computer Networking.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology, graduating with a CGPA of 3.84/4.00 and ranking 3rd out of 50 students. During my undergraduate years and professional career, I actively engaged in problem-solving and software development, participating in over 200 programming contests and solving more than 350 problems across various competitive programming platforms.

My professional journey includes extensive experience in DevOps engineering at Vivasoft Limited, where I worked with cutting-edge cloud technologies including AWS (EC2, EKS, ECS, S3, RDS), Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform, and CI/CD pipelines. I am a Certified Kubernetes Application Developer (CKAD) and HashiCorp Certified Terraform Associate.

I am passionate about research at the intersection of software engineering and artificial intelligence. My current research focuses on distilling small language models for real-world software engineering tasks such as program repair and bug detection, and studying how LLMs are creating technical debt in software systems. I am also highly interested in cutting-edge fields including large language models (LLMs), natural language processing (NLP), and empirical software engineering.
